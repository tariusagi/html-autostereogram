Stereogram

How to view: relax your eyes and look "through" the image as if focusing on something far behind the screen (wall-eyed / parallel viewing), until the two guide dots merge into three and the hidden shape pops out. Keep your head level and give it a few seconds. Bright areas appear nearer by default.
